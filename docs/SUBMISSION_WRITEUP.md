# GenAI Fund outreach prototype

**Approach.** The tool lets a small team run personal outreach on X to people building in AI and robotics. AI does the reading and writing; a person approves every message. You start from topics or from one to three seed accounts you already rate. One X search returns recent posts, a keyword filter ranks the authors, and a model judges the top ten: strong, possible or weak fit, with a reason and the quotes behind it. The model then drafts a short message from what each person actually posted. Every draft lands on a Review page where you approve, edit, ask for a rewrite, skip, or mark the person do-not-contact. Approved messages wait in a queue and are sent one at a time, two minutes apart and at most ten a day. Replies are checked automatically and shown under Needs attention; a reply of STOP blocks further contact.

**Trade-offs.**
- Human approval over autonomy. The brief asks for drafts "to review and approve", and messages go out from the fund's own account.
- Evidence over confidence scores. The app checks every quote the model gives word for word against stored posts and discards the output if one is missing. It cannot prove the model's summary is fair, so the reviewer still reads it.
- Never two messages over always sending. One record per person, a history check before sending, no automatic retries, and a stored request key so a restart cannot resend. An unclear result stops the queue until a person checks.
- Simple over scalable. One Python process and one PostgreSQL database; no message broker or vector database. At ten messages a day this is the smallest design that can be trusted.
- Ready for real outreach, with a test mode. By default any person the app finds can be messaged once a human approves the exact text. One setting, OUTREACH_POLICY=test_allowlist, limits sending to your own test accounts; I used it for my live test.

**Verified and not.** 258 automated tests pass, including real-database concurrency tests and two browser workflows. One controlled live run on real X and OpenAI covered the whole path: connect the account, search, AI judging and drafting with gpt-6-luna, approve, automatic send, reply and sync, for about USD 0.18. The recipient was a second account I control, under the test-list-only rule. Not yet run for real: sending under operator-approved mode, and the stronger model used for rewrites. VERIFICATION.md has the details.

**Costs.** At X list prices a search is about USD 0.45 and a message about USD 0.05. Automatic reply checks are the largest recurring cost, about USD 1.12 per conversation over 14 days at the default interval, and can be slowed or turned off. AI cost is negligible: a search plus ten drafts is roughly 6,000 tokens, about USD 0.001 on gpt-6-luna. Local budget caps stop spending when reached. No paid hosting is needed. Details are in COSTS.md.

**Limits.** A cold DM usually lands in the recipient's message requests, and is refused if they accept messages only from people they follow. Search covers X's seven-day window. DM history covers thirty days. Seed accounts find similar people, not a seed's contacts. One operator and one sender. The daily limit is our caution, not a verified X quota.

**Next steps.** Run operator-approved outreach on real X once the fund decides to, and tune the pace to the account's real DM limits; follow-up reminders; a seed option that reads who a seed interacts with; and moving batch drafting off the web request so large batches don't wait on one page load.

I chose the design, set the safety rules, and reviewed the behaviour through the tests, the browser and a real model run. Inside the product, Gemini or OpenAI models judge fit and draft messages.
